Privacy Policy

Sprouting Minds Abacus ("the app") — last updated October 2026.

Summary

The app does not require an account and does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your light/dark theme preference and your practice settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

Math Riddles and AI

The Math Riddles feature asks Google's Gemini service to write a riddle. The request contains a fixed instruction only; it does not contain your name, contact details or any other information about you. Like any internet request, it is received by Google together with technical information such as your IP address, which Google handles under its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is designed to be safe for children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
