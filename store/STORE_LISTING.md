## Full description (Play 4000 / App Store 4000 chars max)
Sprouting Minds Abacus helps children build speed and confidence in mental math.

Pick a skill, set your own difficulty, and practise at your own pace.

PRACTICE
- Addition and Subtraction: numbers flash on screen one at a time, then you enter the total. Choose how many digits, how many rows and how fast they appear (even half a second).
- Multiplication and Division: beat the clock on timed questions with the number of digits you choose.
- Square Root and Cube Root: find the root of perfect squares and cubes.
- Tables: learn any multiplication table, hear it read aloud, or take a quick table quiz.
- Math Riddles: fresh, AI-generated riddles with four options and a friendly explanation.

TOOLS
- Stopwatch and Countdown Timer for practice sessions.

MADE FOR LEARNING
- Everything is adjustable: digits, number of questions, time per question.
- Instant feedback with the correct answer shown.
- Clean light and dark themes.
- No sign-up, no ads, no in-app purchases.
